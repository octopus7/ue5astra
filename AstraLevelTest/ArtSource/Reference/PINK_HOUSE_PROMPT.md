- 용도: 집과 우물의 개별 모델링 레퍼런스. 아직 모델링하거나 레벨에 배치하지 않은 디자인 자료.
- 배치 의도: 화면상 3시 방향 호숫가, 집 왼쪽에 호수, 화면 앞쪽을 바라보는 현관문, 집 앞 우물.

## 정확한 생성 프롬프트

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: individual 3D modeling reference sheet for AstraLevelTest, a cute low-poly top-down Unreal Engine woodland game.
Primary request: design one small adorable lakeside cottage with a pastel pink gabled roof and a small stone well in its front yard. It sits on the right/east/three-o'clock shore of a woodland lake. The lake is on the LEFT side of the cottage in the main image. The front door faces the camera and the lower edge of the screen; show the entrance clearly.
Style/medium: a clean polished low-poly 3D game asset concept, simple chunky geometry, visibly faceted surfaces, broad readable shapes and restrained hand-painted color variation. Practical for Blender procedural modeling, not intricate realism. Warm cream plaster walls, modest oak framing, pink roof panels with a few large shingle divisions, dark warm wooden front door, a small window on either side, short stone steps and a tiny welcoming porch. Compact single-story footprint, tall friendly gable, no elaborate decoration.
Composition: wide 3:2 landscape modeling sheet. Main large three-quarter elevated orthographic gameplay view occupies about the left 70 percent, looking downward around 58 degrees. House and well fill the view enough to inspect their construction. The house entrance faces the viewer toward the bottom of the image, never away or hidden behind a side wall. Well is in the foreground in front of the entrance, offset to the right so it does not block the door. A short warm ochre dirt path leads from the entrance past the well. The lake is clearly shown to the LEFT with a naturally feathered grassy shoreline, a few reeds and lotus pads, pale clouds reflected in the water. Only a small local grassy patch and two sparse low-poly woodland trees for context, not a whole level.
A narrow clean strip on the right contains three neatly separated small orthographic model views of the SAME design on a warm ivory background: front elevation with visible centered doorway, side elevation showing roof/wall construction, top silhouette including the front well position. Keep proportions, pink roof and window/door placement consistent across views.
Well: simple round low-poly mossy stone rim with a dark open water center, two chunky wooden uprights and a crossbar, visible rope and a small wooden bucket. No canopy over the well, so its opening is readable from above.
Lighting/mood: soft cheerful daylight, broad soft shadows consistent with a 50-degree directional source angle. Olive/sage foliage, mellow yellow-green grass, warm wooden browns, pale rose pink roof, cream walls, blue-green lake.
Constraints: one single cottage design plus one well. Clear isolated modeling shapes, no character, no furniture clutter, no UI, no labels, no title, no watermark. Do not make a ruined building. No photographic microdetail, no fisheye or perspective distortion, no hard black shadows. The roof is PINK, the door faces SCREEN FRONT, and the well is in FRONT of the house.
```
